# 🛡️ UDIE Security Policy

We are committed to maintaining the integrity and availability of the Urban Disruption Intelligence Engine.

⸻

## 🚨 Responsible Disclosure
If you discover a security vulnerability in UDIE, please do not disclose it publicly. We appreciate your assistance in keeping our spatial substrate secure.
- **Email**: `security@udie.io`
- **Response Time**: We aim to acknowledge reports within 48 hours.

⸻

## 🔒 Dimensional Integrity Guards
UDIE's security model focuses on **Signal Legitimacy** and **Computational Stability**:

- **Signal Invariants**: MITM or signal spam is mitigated through multi-source reinforcement and credibility decay kernels.
- **Resource Exhaustion**: The **Law of Bounded Input** prevents malicious actors from triggering expensive spatial compute spikes via pathological route geometries.
- **Data Isolation**: Simulation and production environments are physically separated via table-level and logical isolation.

⸻

## 🚑 Reporting a Vulnerability
When reporting, please include:
1. **Vulnerability Type** (e.g., SQL Injection, H3 Collision, Memory Leak).
2. **Affected Component** (e.g., Ingestion API, Risk Substrate).
3. **Reproduction Steps**.
4. **Potential Impact** on nationwide risk calculations or system availability.
